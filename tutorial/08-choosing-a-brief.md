# 8. Choosing a project brief

Once all three methods pass their gates, choose one brief from the `briefs/`
folder with your supervisor:

1. [Comparing Euler, Heun and RK4 properly](../briefs/01-comparing-methods.md)
2. [Nigeria's population with the logistic model](../briefs/02-nigeria-population.md)
3. [When explicit methods fail: stiffness](../briefs/03-stiffness.md)

Each brief says what you must produce and what counts as your own contribution.
Changing the parameters of a provided problem is not, on its own, a research
contribution: your contribution is the study, the evidence and the
interpretation.

Include in your project appendix: your verification report (printed, or saved
as PDF from your browser), your completed `student/CONTRIBUTION.md`, and your
`steps.py`.
